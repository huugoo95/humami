# Humami Growth & Monetization Roadmap

- **Status:** Working roadmap
- **Created:** 2026-03-31
- **Owner:** Hugo + tenacitas

## 1. Core principle
Humami should not depend on:
- massive SEO volume,
- daily social posting,
- or broad generic recipe publishing.

Humami should be built as:
- a focused library of genuinely useful cooking content,
- a simple funnel from free value to owned audience,
- and a base for small digital products.

## 2. Strategic direction
### Phase 1 focus
**Pizza**

Reason:
- high demand,
- strong beginner pain points,
- good product potential,
- highly visual,
- easier to monetize with a clear low-ticket offer.

### Phase 2 focus
**Bocadillos**

Reason:
- strong differentiation,
- visually attractive,
- good extension of Humami’s identity,
- strong fit for future digital products.

## 3. What Humami is NOT trying to be
- not a generic recipe site with endless content
- not a creator brand that depends on posting every day
- not an Amazon affiliate project first
- not a media machine optimized for vanity metrics

## 4. What Humami IS trying to be
- a trusted source for specific, ambitious-but-accessible home cooking projects
- a website that converts useful content into email subscribers
- a base for focused digital products
- a system that compounds over time without requiring daily content output

## 5. Traffic model
Humami should not rely mainly on organic SEO.
Organic traffic is welcome, but not the primary assumption.

### Primary acquisition model
- a few strong content pieces on the website
- limited, reused distribution content
- direct links into lead magnets and focused landing pages
- optional community/manual sharing where relevant

### Website role
The website is:
- the trust layer
- the archive of high-value content
- the lead capture point
- the product sales point

The website is **not** assumed to be the only acquisition source.

## 6. Funnel model
Recommended funnel:

**content -> website -> lead magnet -> email -> low-ticket product**

### Top of funnel
- recipe pages
- practical guides
- troubleshooting posts
- limited social distribution derived from core pieces

### Middle of funnel
- focused lead magnet
- landing page
- email capture

### Bottom of funnel
- small paid guide / book
- later extensions (additional guides, selected affiliate links)

## 7. Social media policy
Humami should use social media only in a sustainable way.

### Constraints
- no daily posting requirement
- no dependence on algorithm-chasing
- no separate content factory

### Recommended publishing rhythm
- **0 to 2 distribution pieces per week max**
- always derived from existing website content
- no major extra production workflow

### What social is for
- distribution
- discovery
- traffic to site or lead magnet
- validating which topics resonate

### What social is not for
- the main product
- the main source of meaning or momentum
- the place where all effort goes

## 8. Phase 1 content theme: Pizza
### Goal
Build authority around one focused problem space and use it to support a lead magnet + first paid guide.

### Initial content mix
Recommended initial ratio:
- 70% recipes / practical pages
- 30% blog / explanatory pieces

### First 6 foundational pieces
#### Recipes
1. Pizza napolitana base en casa
2. Salsa para pizza napolitana
3. Pizza margarita en horno doméstico
4. Pizza blanca / pizza bianca sencilla
5. Masa de pizza para principiantes (versión más tolerante)

#### Guide / blog
6. 5 errores que arruinan tu pizza en casa

Optional later additions:
- comparativa harina para empezar
- piedra vs steel
- cronograma de fermentación fácil
- cómo mejorar una masa que salió mal

## 9. Lead magnet
### Recommended first lead magnet
**Checklist PDF: “Tu primera pizza napolitana en casa”**

### Goal
Give a beginner enough clarity to start without feeling overwhelmed.

### Recommended content
- equipment minimum viable setup
- ingredients minimum viable setup
- simple preparation timeline
- common mistakes
- quick troubleshooting:
  - dough too sticky
  - dough not fermenting
  - dough bouncing back
  - weak browning

### Why this works
- specific
- immediately useful
- strongly connected to future purchase intent
- easy to create compared to a full book

## 10. First paid product
### Recommendation
Create a **low-ticket digital guide**, not a large book first.

### Working title
**Tu primera pizza napolitana en casa desde cero**

### Recommended price range
- 5€ to 12€

### Recommended scope
- end-to-end beginner method
- dough base recipe
- timeline / planning
- oven adaptation
- troubleshooting
- common decision points
- practical simplification for home cooks

### Product role
This is not meant to maximize revenue immediately.
It is meant to:
- validate monetization,
- test purchase intent,
- and build the first paid asset.

## 11. 6-week execution plan
### Week 1
- lock strategic focus
- define lead magnet structure
- outline first product
- prioritize the first pizza content pieces

### Week 2
- publish recipe 1
- publish recipe 2
- draft lead magnet

### Week 3
- publish guide post (“5 errores…")
- create lead magnet landing page
- connect CTAs from content pages to lead magnet

### Week 4
- publish recipe 3
- publish recipe 4
- build the email welcome sequence

### Week 5
- draft the paid guide
- shape product outline from published material
- prepare sales page

### Week 6
- publish recipe 5
- release first paid guide
- evaluate traffic, signups, and conversion

## 12. Minimal email sequence
Keep this simple.

### Sequence suggestion
1. Deliver lead magnet
2. One practical mistake + fix
3. One useful recipe/guide page
4. Offer the low-ticket guide

No complex newsletter strategy is required at first.

## 13. Metrics that matter
Focus only on:
- visits to priority pages
- lead magnet signups
- email open/click signals
- paid guide sales

Do **not** optimize around:
- likes
- follower count
- vanity engagement

## 14. Phase 2: Bocadillos
Only start this after the pizza phase has produced:
- a small but usable content base,
- a live lead magnet,
- a functioning email flow,
- and the first paid guide.

### Why bocadillos second
- strong visual appeal
- stronger differentiation
- easier to extend into future products
- good fit for Humami’s tone and identity

## 15. Work policy for Hugo
Humami must remain compatible with a busy life.

### Therefore
- no daily publishing requirement
- no multi-platform obsession
- no constant context-switching
- one meaningful weekly content unit is enough

### Sustainable weekly workload
- 1 main content piece
- 1 funnel task
- optional 0–1 small distribution tasks

That is enough.

## 16. Short decision summary
### We choose:
- **Phase 1 = Pizza**
- **Phase 2 = Bocadillos**
- website as the trust + conversion base
- lead magnet early
- low-ticket guide as first monetization
- minimal social distribution only
- no dependence on heavy SEO or daily posting

## 4. Guardrails
- Do not expand Phase 2 (bocadillos) until Phase 1 has:
  - at least 4–6 strong pizza pages,
  - a live lead magnet,
  - a working email flow,
  - a first paid guide in progress or launched.

- Do not create more channels if the current system is not stable.
- Do not rely on daily social media output.
- Prefer one durable content asset over many weak ones.

## 5. Definition of done for Phase 1
Phase 1 is considered “real” when all of the following exist:
- 5 strong pizza pages on the site
- 1 pizza troubleshooting/guide page
- 1 live lead magnet
- 1 working email sequence
- 1 low-ticket paid guide ready or launched
